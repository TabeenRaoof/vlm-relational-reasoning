NOTE ON ROUNDING: the paper's Table 7 reports the healthy regime as
~8s / ~450 per hour, characterizing the regime by the upper end of its
~1-8s spread rather than by its mean. The mean is printed above; both
are marked approximate in the paper. The degraded and Mac rows are the
computed means and match exactly.

Deployment reading (paper Section 6):
  - real-time perception (robot checking left/right before moving): ruled
    out on throughput at these latencies, NOT on accuracy
  - low-frequency monitoring (inspection camera, every few minutes): viable
  - offline batch analysis (on-device review, privacy-motivated): viable
