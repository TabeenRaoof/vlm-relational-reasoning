EFFECT-SIZE SHRINKAGE AND POWER PROJECTION  (paper Section 5.5)

Observed cell accuracies (conditioned on, not estimated):
   high / containment  0.836
   high / projective   0.819
    low / containment  0.828
    low / projective   0.766

pilot interaction estimate       : 0.637 log-odds
confirmatory interaction estimate: 0.266 log-odds
shrinkage                        : 41.8% of the pilot value

A design powered off the pilot estimate (~98% power) in fact had the
power shown at n=2000 below. The pilot's own interaction test was not
significant (p=0.122) — that was the signal not to trust its magnitude.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
TABLE 6 — Monte Carlo power, conditional projection
(true effect assumed = 0.266 log-odds; 4000 replicates; alpha=0.05)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
         n   power
      2000   ~36%  <- this design
      3016   ~52%  <- VSR max balanced (containment pool is the ceiling)
      6000   ~81%  <- adequately powered
      8000   ~90%  <- adequately powered
     10000   ~96%  <- adequately powered

CONSEQUENCE: 80% power needs n ~ 6,000, which is more than twice VSR's
balanced ceiling. Resolving H1 therefore REQUIRES a second dataset
supplying containment volume, plus a fresh pre-registration — adding
items under the existing one would be optional stopping.
